RJ-Wedding Album

22 November 2026 · Bangkok

A private, shared photo album for one wedding. Guests open a link, take photos or short videos in the browser, and the album collects them in one place so the couple can relive the day — and keep a copy in their own Google Drive afterwards.

What the app does

  • Takes photos and 60-second videos directly in the phone browser (no app install).
  • Shows everyone's photos in one shared album, with decorative stamp frames.
  • Lets a guest download a photo with the frame baked in, or the whole album as a ZIP.
  • Archives every file into a Google Drive folder owned by the couple, using the drive.file permission only.

Who it is for

  • Invited guests of the wedding, who receive the private link and event code.
  • The couple, who operate the album and hold the archive.

Privacy in one line

Photos go to the couple's album and their Drive; nothing is sold, published, or shared with advertisers. The public album closes on 22 December 2026.
