Privacy Policy · RJ-Wedding Album

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This page explains what the RJ-Wedding Album app does with the information it handles. The app runs for one private event (the wedding of Rujirata & Jenjobthid, 22 November 2026), is used by the couple and their guests, and is operated by the couple themselves.

1. What the app accesses

  • Photos and short videos that a guest chooses or records inside the app, and the guest name they type.
  • Google Drive access: only with the drive.file permission, which lets the app create and read files it created itself. The app cannot see or change any other file in the owner's Drive.

2. Where the data is stored

  • Uploads are stored in Cloudflare R2 (object storage) so guests can view the shared album.
  • A copy is archived into a Google Drive folder owned by the couple (Google Account of the event owner).
  • No database of personal profiles is kept. The only personal data is the name a guest types, stored alongside their media.

3. How the data is used

  • To show the shared album to the couple and their guests.
  • To archive the photos and videos so the couple keeps them after the event.
  • Nothing else: the media is not sold, not published, and not shared with advertisers or any third party.

4. Google user data (Limited Use)

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to archive the files created by this app, is never transferred to third parties, is never used for advertising, and is never read by humans except when the owner themselves opens the files.

5. Retention and deletion

  • The public album is switched off on 22 December 2026, and the files in Cloudflare R2 are deleted after that date.
  • The archive in Google Drive stays in the couple's own Drive; they can delete it at any time like any other folder.
  • A guest who wants their photo removed can ask the couple, who can delete it from the album.

6. Access

  • The album is reachable with a private link plus an event code shared with guests.
  • Access to Google Drive is limited to the couple's own Google Account (OAuth). No one else holds the credentials.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them beyond photos taken at the event.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above changes with it.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or a deletion request: please contact the couple via the email shown on the app's consent screen.
